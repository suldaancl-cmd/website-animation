# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Founders and operators in frozen, technical, or overlooked B2B markets — Arctic
logistics, maritime, deep-tech, industrial, research. They are precise people who
distrust hype. Their context: evaluating whether a studio understands a serious,
unglamorous market well enough to make it unmistakable. The job to be done:
"prove, in ten seconds of scrolling, that this studio brings clarity and warmth
to a cold category without dumbing it down."

Emotional goal: calm confidence. The site should feel like a well-engineered
instrument, not a sales pitch.

## Product Purpose

Kaltuun is a small design studio that brings colour — brand, identity, motion —
to companies operating at the edge of their markets. The site exists to win
"start a project" enquiries from serious founders by demonstrating taste and
restraint rather than describing it. Success = a qualified founder reaches the
contact section convinced the studio is precise, in-house, and unlike the
generic agencies they've already dismissed.

## Brand Personality

Three words: **precise, calm, warm-at-the-core**. Voice is plain and confident —
short declaratives, no adjective pile-ups, no agency filler. The motif is literal:
a cold, white arctic world that carries living colour (the aurora). Restraint on
the surface, one moment of colour as the payoff.

## Anti-references

- **Corporate agency stock.** No stock photos of people pointing at screens, no
  "we craft digital experiences" filler, no carousel sliders, no blue gradients
  used as decoration.
- **Loud crypto / Web3.** No neon-on-black, no chrome 3D blobs, no hype
  maximalism, no glassmorphism-everywhere.
- **Sterile corporate-clean.** No safe white + single-blue Helvetica blandness
  with zero personality. Precision is not the same as sterility — the aurora
  colour is the warmth that keeps it from going cold.

## Design Principles

1. **Cold surface, warm signal.** The default world is restrained arctic
   near-neutral; colour appears as a deliberate payoff (aurora, the word
   "colour"), never as wallpaper.
2. **Show the craft, don't claim it.** Motion, 3D, and typographic control are
   the proof. The copy stays quiet because the execution is loud.
3. **Precision over volume.** Measured spacing, restrained type, one idea per
   section. Calm beats clever.
4. **Respect the reader's intelligence.** Plain language, no hype, no
   over-explaining. These founders dismiss anything that talks down to them.
5. **Every effect earns its place.** Bloom, parallax, scroll-spin exist to serve
   the "colour to the ice" idea, not to demo features.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG AA for text contrast; the arctic light theme must keep body and
  label text readable against the bright background and over glass surfaces.
- Honour `prefers-reduced-motion`: scroll-driven camera, parallax, and aurora
  motion should reduce to a static or minimal state when requested.
- Don't rely on colour alone to carry meaning; the aurora is atmosphere, not
  information.
